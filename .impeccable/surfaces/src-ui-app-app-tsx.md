## Direction contract

THESIS: The salary is water let into a hillside of bancales in its turn: expenses, you, emergency fund, investment. The month is drawn as a terraced cascade where each terrace's length is its exact share of net income and water visibly drops from lip to lip. It refuses the balance-card-plus-donut default and the incumbent's sage-forest calm.

OWN-WORLD: Lime-washed chalk ground (neutral, not cream), stone-black ink, acequia blue carries water and every action. Four crop colours own whole regions and are law everywhere, always in this order: stone wall (gastos), Valencian orange (para ti), golden rice (colchón), huerta green (inversión). Dry, unassigned earth is hatched, never hidden; a deficit cracks in honest red. The hero is a deep acequia-blue field. Surfaces are worn-stone slabs with subtly irregular radii, no hairline card borders; a thin water channel links navigation and states. Funnel Display for figures, Funnel Sans for text.

STORY: The user sees in one glance how their salary irrigates each priority in order, trusts it because each terrace is an exact share, and on Día a día reads today's allowance as the water left in their own terrace, then logs a purchase in seconds.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Overview, desktop: heading left, primary action as a water-blue pill top right. The hero spans the content width: deep blue field, the terraced cascade on the left (about 60%), the four priorities on the right as large figures with crop swatch, amount and share, closing with Margen libre. Below it, the four headline figures sit in one stone strip. Phone: cascade full width, figures stacked under it. Signature interaction: on load and on every plan change, water runs along the acequia and drops over each terrace lip in priority order (staggered path draw), terraces fill left to right.
